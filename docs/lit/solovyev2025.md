# solovyev2025

Held, forty pages, read in full against the PDF on 2026-09-14. The
Section 5.3 numbers read twice.

## What it covers

Line-profile asymmetry from nonresonant (NR) terms in the QED scattering
amplitude: a rigorous derivation of the Lorentz profile and its NR
extension (Section 2), NR corrections to total and differential cross
sections (Section 3), angular correlations of the quantum-interference
effect (QIE) in one-photon spectroscopy of H, muonic H and 3He
(Section 4), the two-photon case for H 2s-ns/nd, 1s-3s and He 2^3S-n^3D
(Section 5), thermal (blackbody) line broadening as it enters the NR
shift (Section 5.4), and the conclusion that beyond the resonance
approximation only the line profile, not a transition frequency, is the
measurable quantity (Section 6).

## The claims, with pages

1. The leading NR shift of the line maximum from a neighbouring resonance
   a' at separation Delta from the resonant level a of width Gamma_a:
   delta_NR = [f(a, a') / f(a, a)] Gamma_a^2 / (4 Delta), eq. (69) p. 13,
   with the next order proportional to Gamma^4 / Delta^3, eq. (70). The
   two-photon form has the same structure with two-photon amplitudes,
   eq. (97) p. 24.
2. The angular dependence for dipole photons reduces to 1 + 3 cos 2 theta
   and vanishes at the magic angles 54.7 and 125.3 degrees, for both
   experiment types (p. 17. P. 25 for two photons).
3. Worked one-photon case, H 2s-4p: Gamma_a = 1.2941e7 Hz, Delta =
   1367433.3 kHz, Gamma/Delta = 0.00946 (p. 15). Corrections of 60.7127,
   -30.3563, 30.3563, 6.0713 and -15.1782 Hz by final state (Table 1
   p. 15), and channel-dependent line maxima differing by more than 50 kHz
   against a 3 kHz experimental error (eq. 75 p. 16).
4. Two-photon H: delta_NR(2s-nd, F = 2) = 967.75, 296.48, 127.31 and
   38.38 Hz for n = 4, 6, 8, 12 (eqs. 99 to 102, pp. 25 to 26). Delta_NR(1s-3s) = -225.61 (1 + 3 cos 2 theta) Hz (eq. 98 p. 25). For
   the 2s-8d quench-type experiment the correction is -1174.02 Hz against
   a 2 kHz experimental error (p. 28).
5. Section 5.3 (pp. 28 to 29), He 2^3S1-3^3D1: with the natural width
   11.35(6) MHz the NR shift is 0.0124(4) MHz, eq. (111). With the
   observed width Gamma_exp = Gamma_nat + Gamma_pb + Gamma_tt, eq. (112),
   where the pressure broadening is 35.7(1.7) MHz per Torr (helium in
   helium, their ref 43) and the transit contribution about 0.07 MHz,
   Gamma_exp runs from 13.2(4) to 29.2(4) MHz over 0.05 to 0.5 Torr and
   delta_NR from 0.016(1) to 0.082(19) MHz. At 0.151 Torr it is 0.027 MHz.
   For 2^3S-4^3D and 5^3D the broadening coefficients are 68.1(2.7) and
   78.5(2.7) MHz per Torr and the corrections reach 2.65(2) and 6.6(4) MHz
   (Table 7 p. 30).
6. Section 5.4 (p. 30): the blackbody-induced width Gamma_a^beta =
   (4/3) e^2 sum_n |<a|r|n>|^2 n_beta(omega_an) omega_an^3, eq. (113),
   with n_beta the Planck occupation, eq. (114). Table 8 gives H ns and nd
   at 300 K, e.g. 12s: 5718 Hz thermal against 4.77e4 Hz natural, ratio
   x = 2 Gamma^beta / Gamma^nat = 0.24. The statement on p. 30 is that
   the NR shift's proportionality to Gamma^2 makes it sensitive to the
   width observed in the experiment, so every broadening enters it.
7. The conclusion (pp. 18 to 19, 30 to 31): beyond the resonance
   approximation the line profile is the measurable object and the
   transition frequency is extracted from it by a procedure whose
   asymmetry treatment is part of the result. Fixing the detected
   hyperfine sublevel of the final state makes the correction
   angle-independent and subtractable (p. 18).

## Validity

A rigorous QED treatment for one- and two-electron systems, where the
neighbouring resonances are fine and hyperfine partners of the resonant
level and the widths are natural. Its numbers are for H, muonic H and He
and transfer to rubidium only through the formula.

## What this record takes from it

1. **The QIE scale for 5S-6S, and why it is closed.** The nearest
   partner of the resonant 6S(F) level is the other 6S hyperfine level, at
   Delta = 717.195 MHz for 85Rb and 1614.709 MHz for 87Rb
   (`rb5s6s/constants.py`), with Gamma_6S = 1 / (2 pi 45.57 ns) =
   3.49 MHz. Eq. (69)'s Gamma^2 / (4 Delta) is then 4.2 kHz for 85Rb and
   1.9 kHz for 87Rb as the upper scale of an excitation-branch QIE, before
   the amplitude ratio (calculated here). And that ratio is zero at this order:
   for two photons of one shared linear polarisation between J = 1/2
   states only the rank-0 operator survives (the polarisation argument in
   `constants.py`), so the Delta F = +-1 branch that would interfere is
   not driven at all. What the review leaves open for this line is the
   interference in the cascade emission that it names on p. 19 (its ref
   90), which the four-peak detection on the 795 nm cascade could carry
   and which is not quantified here. Against the light shift S0 =
   0.35 MHz at 225 mW and the adopted waist (`constants.py`) the record's
   asymmetry is at least 80 times any QIE scale, and against the ruler's
   few-kilohertz floor ([steck_rb](steck_rb.md)) a QIE would sit at the
   floor. The reading in [anikin2022](anikin2022.md) stands, with one
   correction to its wording: the review's shifts are of order the
   natural width times Gamma / (4 Delta) times an amplitude ratio, not of
   order the natural width times a ratio, which overstates them by
   Gamma / (4 Delta), about 1e-3 here.
2. **The width budget as a sum, with the pressure lever.** Eq. (112) and
   Fig. 10 (p. 29) are the shape of this record's own width budget,
   natural plus collisional plus transit plus laser, and of the
   extrapolation to zero pressure that reads a shift off a pressure
   ladder. The 35.7(1.7) MHz per Torr is a helium-in-helium coefficient
   and bears on nothing in beta_self. What transfers is that any
   asymmetry-driven centre shift scales with the square of the observed
   width, so a centre read at 130 C and 225 mW, where the record's widths
   are largest, carries the largest such term.
3. **Blackbody.** Eq. (113) is the same rate form as
   `rb5s6s/blackbody.py`'s `bbr_rate` (the spontaneous rate times the
   Planck occupation). Table 8 gives no rubidium number and the record's
   6S blackbody figures (`results/blackbody_channels.csv`) are not
   touched.
4. **Bibliographic.** This is the published form of
   [anikin2022](anikin2022.md), whose verify flag says the Physics Reports
   version is not held. It is now, and that note is owed an update in the
   wave that files this one.
