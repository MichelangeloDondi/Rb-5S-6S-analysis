# cao2025

**VERIFIED, 2026-08-02**, full text read.

**What it measures.** Rb self-broadening of the 85Rb 5S(1/2)(F=2) to
5D(3/2)(F''=1,2,3,4) Doppler-free two-photon line, driven by one 778 nm laser
through a virtual level and detected on the 795, 762 and 420 nm cascade
fluorescence. The cell is sealed pure Rb with no buffer gas, so the pressure
axis is Rb's own vapour pressure and the coefficient is self-broadening by
construction, although the paper only ever says "pressure broadening".

**The numbers.**

| quantity | value | convention |
|---|---|---|
| self-broadening slope | 40 ± 0.54 kHz/mTorr | FWHM, stated |
| zero-pressure intercept | 1.03 ± 0.1 MHz | FWHM, stated |
| 5D(3/2) natural width, for comparison | ~0.97 MHz | FWHM |
| collisional shift | not reported | — |

**The convention is explicit, which is why this note is worth its length.**
The abstract labels the 1.03 MHz "full-width at half-maximum (FWHM)", and the
same figure supplies the fit whose slope is the 40 kHz/mTorr. Most of the
neighbouring literature leaves HWHM against FWHM to be inferred, a factor of
two that silently corrupts any comparison table. Here it does not have to be.

**Converted to this repository's units.** Density is not measured. It is
computed from cell temperature through a vapour-pressure formula over a
100 to 210 C scan, so temperature and pressure covary and the conversion
factor moves about 23% across their range. At 423 K, the temperature of their
reference spectra, n = 2.28e13 cm^-3 per mTorr, so

    40 kHz/mTorr  ->  0.0018 MHz per 1e12 cm^-3  (FWHM)

with roughly 0.0015 to 0.0020 across the ends of their scan. Quote it as a
range, not a number.

**What it does not do.** It says nothing about 5S-6S. The 6S(1/2) level
appears exactly once in the whole paper, as a waypoint in a decay cascade,
with no measurement attached. So it neither confirms nor contradicts this
archive's 5S-6S bound directly, and the claim that the 993 nm line's
coefficients have only ever been bounded stands untouched.

**Why it still matters here.** It is a real measured entry, at 1.3% relative
precision, on the rung immediately below the one this programme works on, by
the same group and method as [wang2025](wang2025.md) on the rung above. The
two together turn "the 5D/7S series is missing its 6S entry" from an assertion
into a table with numbers in it. See [BIG_PICTURE](../BIG_PICTURE.md) §1.

**One methodological caveat to carry if this is cited as a benchmark.** The
density is inferred from temperature, never measured independently, and the
vapour-pressure reference cited is Steck's Rb-87 data document for a 85Rb
cell. The formula is effectively isotope-independent so this is a labelling
quirk rather than an error, but there is no independent density check behind
the x-axis.
