# cao2025

Held. Verified against the full text.

## The system and method

Measures Rb self-broadening on the 85Rb 5S(1/2)(F=2) to 5D(3/2)(F''=1,2,3,4) Doppler-free two-photon line, driven by one 778 nm laser through a virtual level and detected on the 795, 762 and 420 nm cascade fluorescence. The cell is sealed pure Rb with no buffer gas, so the pressure axis is Rb's own vapour pressure and the coefficient is self-broadening by construction, though the paper states only "pressure broadening."

## The numbers

| quantity | value | convention |
|---|---|---|
| self-broadening slope | 40 ± 0.54 kHz/mTorr | FWHM, stated |
| zero-pressure intercept | 1.03 ± 0.1 MHz | FWHM, stated |
| 5D(3/2) natural width, for comparison | ~0.97 MHz | FWHM |
| collisional shift | not reported | n/a |

The FWHM convention is stated explicitly, unlike much of this literature, where HWHM against FWHM is left to be inferred, a factor of two that can corrupt a comparison. Density is not measured directly: it is computed from cell temperature through a vapour-pressure formula over a 100-210 C scan, so the conversion factor moves by about 23% across the range. At 423 K, the reference temperature, n = 2.28e13 cm^-3 per mTorr, giving 40 kHz/mTorr -> 0.0018 MHz per 1e12 cm^-3 (FWHM), ranging from about 0.0015 to 0.0020 across the scan.

## Scope and validity

The 6S(1/2) level appears once in the paper, as a waypoint in a decay cascade, with no measurement attached: the paper neither confirms nor contradicts the existing 5S-6S bound. Density is inferred from temperature rather than measured independently, and the vapour-pressure reference cited is Steck's Rb-87 data document applied to a 85Rb cell, a labelling detail and not an error, since the formula is effectively isotope-independent, though there is no independent density check behind the pressure axis.

## Use in this record

A measured self-broadening coefficient at 1.3% relative precision on the two-photon line immediately below 5S-6S (778 nm, 5S-5D3/2), by the same group and method as [wang2025](wang2025.md) on the line above. See [BIG_PICTURE](../BIG_PICTURE.md) §1.
