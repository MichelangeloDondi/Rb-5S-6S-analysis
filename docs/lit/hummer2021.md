# hummer2021

**Held 2026-07-30**, not yet read in full. Fetched from arXiv (2006.12855) after
an external literature pass named it "the nearest un-applied candidate
mechanism" for the Patterson/Liu unexplained-width question
([patterson2018](patterson2018.md)).

Skimmed only: it is about atoms bound to (adsorbed on, or trapped at) a
nanofibre surface, probed via the guided mode, from the Rauschenbeutel/
Schneeweiss group at Innsbruck and Berlin. Whether it supplies a
position-dependent decay-rate or width mechanism distinct from
[klimovducloy2004](klimovducloy2004.md)'s free-space cylinder result, or from
Sagué's $\gamma_{\rm free}(r) + \gamma_{\rm guid}(r)$ split, has **not been
checked**. That check is the reason it was fetched and is still open.
