# raj2026

Held. Pages 1-3 and 5-8 verified against the arXiv preprint (v1, 1 July
2026).

## The system

Two-photon Rydberg excitation of cold 87Rb (780 nm then 480 nm) to nD5/2,
n = 27-40, through the evanescent field of a 370 nm silica nanofibre, with
added fibre-guided dipole-trapping light: counterpropagating 1064 nm beams
at about 2 mW each (attractive) and a single 762 nm beam at about 1 mW
(repulsive), giving a trap minimum near 400 nm from the surface at 0.17 mK.

With both trapping wavelengths on, the Rydberg spectrum broadens and
develops extra features over tens of minutes, suppressed by an external
oscillating electric field. The authors attribute this to surface charge
from Rydberg-ground-state collisional ionisation enhanced by the trapping
light: about 16 ionisations per second for 40D5/2, about 14,000 charges
over fifteen minutes.

## The model

A multilevel Lindblad master equation over eight levels (ground,
intermediate, and six Rydberg m_j states) with DC polarizabilities from
ARC treats the DC field as a free parameter fit to the data, returning
2.45 V/cm at 300 nm falling to 2.35 V/cm at 500 nm for 35D5/2, and 1.5 to
1.4 V/cm at 400-500 nm for 40D5/2. A separate charge model reproduces
these fields with about 5×10⁵ adsorbed Rb dipoles (10 Debye) and about 65
surface electrons.

The authors state: "As our model does not account for all broadening
mechanisms in the system, such as Rydberg-Rydberg interactions, atom-ion or
atom-electron collisions, Rydberg-ion or Rydberg-electron interactions, the
result only matches the experimental data qualitatively." AC light shifts
from the trapping beams were left out of the model because no shift of the
Rydberg resonance was observed.

lists. The same platform appears in [gokhroo2022](gokhroo2022.md) (an
