# raj2026

Read 2026-08-22 from the arXiv PDF (v1, 1 July 2026), pages 1-3 and 5-8. Same
group and platform as [gokhroo2022](gokhroo2022.md) and
[vylegzhanin2023](vylegzhanin2023.md). Robert Low of Stuttgart is a co-author,
which is a Rydberg group outside the unit's earlier author lists.

## What they did

Two-photon Rydberg excitation of cold 87Rb, 780 nm then 480 nm, to nD5/2 with
n from 27 to 40, through the evanescent field of a **370 nm** silica nanofibre.
The new ingredient is fibre-guided **dipole trapping light**: counterpropagating
1064 nm at about 2 mW each, attractive, plus a single 762 nm beam at about
1 mW, repulsive, giving a trap minimum near **400 nm from the surface at
0.17 mK**.

With BOTH trapping wavelengths on, the Rydberg spectrum broadens and grows
extra features over tens of minutes. An external oscillating electric field
suppresses them. They attribute this to **surface charge**, and identify
**Rydberg-ground-state collisional ionisation, enhanced by the trapping light**,
as the generating mechanism: about 16 ionisations per second for 40D5/2, some
14,000 charges in a fifteen-minute exposure.

## The model, and the sentence that matters most here

A multilevel Lindblad master equation over eight levels, with the ground,
intermediate and six Rydberg m_j states, DC polarizabilities from ARC.

**The DC field is a FREE PARAMETER**, in their words *"treated as a free
parameter to match the model with the experimental data"*. It returns
**2.45 V/cm at 300 nm falling to 2.35 V/cm at 500 nm** for 35D5/2, and
1.5 to 1.4 V/cm at 400-500 nm for 40D5/2. A separate charge model reproduces
those fields with about 5e5 adsorbed Rb dipoles of 10 Debye plus about 65
electrons on the surface.

And then, verbatim:

> "As our model does not account for all broadening mechanisms in the system,
> such as Rydberg-Rydberg interactions, atom-ion or atom-electron collisions,
> Rydberg-ion or Rydberg-electron interactions, the result only matches the
> experimental data qualitatively."

They also state that **AC light shifts from the trapping beams were left out of
the model**, because no shift of the Rydberg resonance was observed.

## Why this sits beside our own record

The 2023 paper excluded DC Stark shifts for want of a way to quantify them.
This paper supplies one, and the way it does so is to **extract a field by
fitting a lineshape model whose broadening is admittedly incomplete**. That is
the same structure as this record's own kernel problem: several mechanisms
entering one observable, a parameter recovered by fitting, and the question of
how much that parameter moves when the model class is wrong.

Three papers from this group now reach a lineshape they can describe but not
fully model: the unmodelled pushing dip of [gokhroo2022](gokhroo2022.md), the
empirical skewed Gaussian of [vylegzhanin2023](vylegzhanin2023.md) absorbing an
AC Stark shift and a surface interaction together, and a qualitative multilevel
fit here. See also [patrick2025](patrick2025.md) for the same physics measured
on glass vapour cells at NIST.
