# wang2022

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## The experiment

An inertia-sensitive atom interferometer guided inside a "22-cm-long negative
curvature hollow-core photonic crystal" fibre, with an interferometer time of
twenty milliseconds. The fibre description is quoted verbatim. The improvement over the group's earlier fibre
interferometer is attributed to in-fibre cooling: the abstract reports
"gray molasses and delta-kick cooling to cool atoms from 32" microkelvin to
below one microkelvin in four milliseconds.

## The sentence that carries the design

"The in-fiber cooling overcomes the inevitable heating during the atom loading
process and allows a shallow guiding optical potential to minimize
decoherence." That is the answer to `xin2019`'s differential light shift, and
it is a design rule this record's guided forecast does not yet carry: the trap
depth is chosen against the line, not only against the atom count.

## What a cold sample does to this record's widths

The transit width goes as the square root of the temperature over the waist,
which `constants.transit_fwhm_from_w0` computes. Evaluated for 85Rb:

| sample | 64 um | 40 um | 22 um | 16 um |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| warm cell, 130 C | 0.9687 | 1.550 | 2.818 | 3.875 |
| in-fibre pre-cool, 32 uK | 2.729e-4 | 4.367e-4 | 7.939e-4 | 1.092e-3 |
| after in-fibre cooling, 1 uK | 4.824e-5 | 7.719e-5 | 1.403e-4 | 1.930e-4 |

Megahertz, against a natural width of 3.493 MHz. At the campaign's tight waist
the warm transit width is comparable to the natural width and is the term that
makes the tight configuration unattractive. On a cold platform it is about a
twenty-thousandth of the warm width and an eighteen-thousandth of the natural
one, and stops being a term at all. The first of those two is parameter-free:
the width goes as the square root of the temperature, so the ratio is the
square root of 403.15 K over one microkelvin whatever the waist and whichever
isotope.

That is the whole reason this record's identifiability problem is a WARM-cell
problem. The published correlation between the collisional and transit widths
is -0.958, and a sample at a microkelvin removes one of the two.

## What it costs

Atoms. The cold guided rows of `results/platform_twins.csv` carry four to five
orders of magnitude fewer atoms than the warm ones, and `xin2018` measures the
host group's own in-fibre number at about ten thousand. That trade is read in
[xin2018](xin2018.md) against the row's own cells and is not resolved here.
