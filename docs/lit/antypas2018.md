# antypas2018

Demonstrates elimination of ac-Stark-induced lineshape asymmetry in a weak atomic transition in ytterbium driven by an intense standing-wave field. The held manuscript describes two methods that restore the symmetric profile of the 174Yb 1S0 to 3D1 line in an atomic beam, at the optical power of the group's parity-violation studies.

## Use in this record

The asymmetry-elimination technique described here is inverted in this analysis into a reference-free asymmetry observable, rather than removed as a systematic.
