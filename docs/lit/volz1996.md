# volz1996

Held. Verified against the PDF, ten pages, on 2026-09-10.

## The measurement

Beam-gas-laser spectroscopy on alkali atoms and helium. The rubidium 5s-5p row
of the paper's own table reads, verbatim, "Rb 5s-5p This work (95) BGLS 27.70
(4) 26.24 (4) 53.64 (8)", so `tau(5P1/2) = 27.70(4) ns` and
`tau(5P3/2) = 26.24(4) ns`.

## Why this record needed it

`rb5s6s.polarizability.LINES_5S` carries the reduced dipole elements the whole
5S polarizability sum is built on, and the two that dominate it are the D lines
at 4.231(3) and 5.978(5) atomic units. Every absolute statement this record
makes about `Delta_alpha` rests on them, and until this paper was read they
were adopted from a secondary source rather than checked against the
measurement they come from. The external readings of 2026-09-06 named that
exactly: one real gap, the experimental 5s-5p elements, taken purely on trust.

## The round trip, which is the point

Through `A = omega^3 d^2 / (3 pi eps0 hbar c^3 (2J+1))`, with the term
energies this package already holds:

| level | element, a.u. | lifetime from it | Volz measured | agreement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5P1/2 | 4.231(3) | 27.704(39) ns | 27.70(4) ns | 0.1 sigma |
| 5P3/2 | 5.978(5) | 26.240(44) ns | 26.24(4) ns | 0.0 sigma |

So the adopted elements reproduce this paper's measured lifetimes to a tenth
of a sigma and to zero, with the propagated uncertainties matching the
measured ones. That is what one expects if they were derived from it through a
compilation, and it is a strong consistency check.

**It is not, on its own, a provenance.** `rb5s6s.polarizability.LINES_5S`
carries no citation, and no note in `docs/lit/` states where 4.231(3) and
5.978(5) were adopted from, so the identity is inferred from agreement and not
traced. Naming the compilation would settle it and is owed. Until it is, the
5S side of the chain is anchored by a consistency check and not by a cited
line of descent.

## What it does not settle

It anchors the 5S side only. The 6S side rests on the sum over states of
`safronova2004` and `safronovaSS2011`, and the 6S lifetime on `gomez2005`.
Nothing here bears on the sign dispute, which `docs/CLAIMS.md` settles at 210
sigma from the 6S lifetime by a route that does not pass through these
elements at all.
