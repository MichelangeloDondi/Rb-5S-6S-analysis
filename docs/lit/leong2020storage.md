# leong2020storage

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## The result

The abstract states, verbatim, that the authors "load cold Rb atomic vapor
into an optical trap inside a hollow-core photonic crystal" fibre, store the
phase of the light in a long-lived spin wave and retrieve it after a
controllable delay using electromagnetically induced transparency, and that
"We achieve over 50 ms of storage time".

## What it settles for this record

Nothing about linewidth, and that is the point worth writing down. A guided
two-photon measurement of the kind this record forecasts needs the atoms to
stay coherent for the excitation and the detection, which is microseconds. A
platform that holds a ground-state coherence for fifty milliseconds is not
close to limiting it. So of the three costs a guided arm might carry, atom
number, differential light shift and coherence time, the third is not one.

The differential light shift is `xin2019` and it is real. The atom number is
`xin2018` and it is the binding one.
