# wang2025

**VERIFIED, 2026-08-02**, full text read.

**What it measures.** Rb 5S(1/2) to 7S(1/2) by monochromatic two-photon
excitation with a single 760 nm laser, read out on five simultaneous
fluorescence channels at 780, 741, 795, 728 and about 420 nm through a filter
array into one PMT. The argument for multi-channel detection is
signal-to-noise: the branching ratios normalised to 780 nm run
1 : 0.64 : 0.52 : 0.35 : 0.20, so the conventional single 420 nm channel
throws most of the decay away.

**The numbers.**

| quantity | value | convention |
|---|---|---|
| self-broadening slope | 0.32 ± 0.01 MHz/mTorr | **not stated** |
| zero-pressure intercept | 1.08 ± 0.02 MHz | not stated |
| 7S natural width, quoted for comparison | ~0.9 MHz | not stated |
| collisional shift | not reported | — |

Cell is natural-abundance Rb with no buffer gas, 100 to 170 C for the
broadening scan, density again computed from temperature rather than measured.

**Converted, with the caveat attached.** At 423 K the same arithmetic as in
[cao2025](cao2025.md) gives

    0.32 MHz/mTorr  ->  0.014 MHz per 1e12 cm^-3

which sits a factor 8 above the 5D(3/2) entry from the same group. That factor
is the interesting part, and it is exactly the quantity the missing convention
could halve or double. Treat the 8 as indicative until someone pins HWHM
against FWHM here.

**Where this archive's 5S-6S numbers fall against it.** The per-peak fitted
central values here are 0.026 to 0.045 MHz per 1e12 cm^-3, which is 1.8 to 3.2
times the 7S entry, and the headline 95% bound is 0.21 to 0.43, some 15 to 31
times above it. Two things follow, and the second is the useful one. The bound
has a great deal of headroom, as a bound should. And the fitted centrals sit
*above* the 7S rung even though 6S is the more compact state, which is an
independent sign, from outside this archive entirely, that those centrals are
the floor this repository already argues they are rather than resolved
collisions. The internal evidence for that reading is the lever test, where the
fitted width rises only 1.47 times across a 52.5 times density span.

**It is also a competitor, and the framing should say so.** The paper's closing
claim is that its results "establish a feasible foundation for developing
optical frequency standards based on the Rb 5S - 7S transition". So 5S-7S is
being advanced for the same application class this programme's line sits in.
That is context [BIG_PICTURE](../BIG_PICTURE.md) §1 should carry, not hide.

**On 5S-6S.** Cited once, as [nieddu2019](nieddu2019.md), jointly with another
reference, purely to support a statement about circular-polarization selection
rules cancelling two-photon amplitudes. No linewidth, no broadening, no shift,
no comparison. The 993 nm gap is not filled by this paper.
