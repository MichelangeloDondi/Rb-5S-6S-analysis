# martin2019

VERIFIED, 2026-07-29.

Reports a differential-polarizability method for the ac-Stark shift of the rubidium 5S-5D two-photon transition, used elsewhere for other alkali two-photon differential-polarizability measurements, including 5S-6S.

## Internal inconsistencies

The abstract and Table II quote the ac-Stark coefficient as +2.30(4)e-13 per mW/mm^2, while Fig. 5's caption reports the measured value as -2.5(2)e-13, the same quantity with opposite printed sign, confirmed against the typeset PDF.

Equation (2) is printed as alpha(omega,J) = -(2/3(2J+1)) sum_J' omega_J'J |<J|d|J'>|^2 / (omega^2_J'J - omega^2), and Eq. (21) repeats the leading minus sign. As printed, a ground state below resonance would be negatively polarizable, which contradicts the measured alpha_5S(DC) = +318.79(1.42). Whether the tabulated values follow the printed equation or the standard sign convention cannot be determined from the PDF alone.

## Use in this record

The printed sign in this paper is not reliable enough on its own to settle differential-polarizability sign conventions in this literature, including an unresolved sign question for Delta-alpha at 993 nm elsewhere in the same body of work.
