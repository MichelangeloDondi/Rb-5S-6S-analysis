# rajasree2020spin

Held. DOI verified against the publisher PDF.

## The system

Two-photon excitation of the rubidium 5S-6S transition, in warm vapour
with paraxial beams and in cold 87Rb around a 400 nm optical nanofibre.
The rate is proportional to the squared degree of linear polarization and
vanishes for circular polarization, confirmed by a null test under a fixed
frequency lock. Evanescent-field excitation around the nanofibre reaches
25-40 counts/ms, against a nonparaxial minimum of about 13% calculated
versus 25% measured.

A K = 0, 1, 2 decomposition of the two-photon operator provides the
published basis for treating the transition amplitude as scalar-only under
these conditions.

## Use in this record

For this programme's 5S-6S transition, circular polarization suppresses
the whole two-photon signal rather than isolating the Doppler-free line
from the pedestal. Orthogonal linear polarization isolates the cross term
driving the Doppler-free line from the same-beam terms driving the
pedestal.
