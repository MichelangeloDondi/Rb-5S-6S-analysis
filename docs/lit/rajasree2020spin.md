# rajasree2020spin

(held; DOI read off the publisher PDF). OUR line, two configs: warm vapor paraxial (rate proportional to the squared degree of linear polarization, ZERO for circular — the polarization amplitude law and the fixed-lock session extinction null-test) and cold 87Rb around a 400 nm ONF (evanescent-field 5S-6S at 25-40 counts/ms — nanofibre feasibility evidence; nonparaxial minimum ~13% theory / 25% practice). The K=0,1,2 two-photon operator decomposition is the published in-lineage basis for the M10 scalar-only degeneracy law.

**Read again 2026-08-16 for the next campaign's polarisation design, and it corrected a proposal.** A draft of PLAN section 10c had circular polarisation removing the Doppler pedestal while keeping the Doppler-free line, which would have made a line-only block. This paper's law says otherwise: the rate follows the squared degree of LINEAR polarisation and vanishes for circular, so circular light removes the whole two-photon signal rather than one component of it. What it is good for is an EXTINCTION NULL, the atoms switched off by polarisation with nothing else in the apparatus altered. The isolation that does work is orthogonal linear, which kills the cross term driving the Doppler-free line and leaves the same-beam terms driving the pedestal. PLAN 10c.9 now records both, and cites this note for the law.
