# kuan2016

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## The result

The abstract reports a light-dragging experiment in an electromagnetically
induced transparent cold atomic ensemble that, verbatim, "enhance the dragging
effect by at least three orders of magnitude" over previous experiments, and an
atom-based velocimeter with, verbatim, "sensitivity two orders of magnitude
higher than the velocity width" of the atomic medium used.

## Why it is here

The sentence about the velocity width is the transferable one. Their figure of
merit is a fraction of the ensemble's own Doppler width, and a cold ensemble
wins by making that width small. This record's bound is limited by widths it
cannot separate, and the campaign's proposed answers are all ways of changing
a width. So the host group's cold-platform experience is directly the
experience this record's campaign needs, even though the measurement is a
different one.

## The warm counterpart

`chen2020` is the same idea in a room-temperature vapour, where the dispersion
rather than the narrowness carries the sensitivity.
