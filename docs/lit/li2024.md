## The system

A "resonance-offset" locking approach that cancels the light shift and the vapor-cell temperature shift together in a compact atomic clock, using the offset-lock shift to counteract the atomic resonance shift from changes in cell temperature and light power. Demonstrated on the 778 nm rubidium two-photon optical frequency standard. From the Wuhan Institute (Chinese Academy of Sciences Innovation Academy for Precision Measurement).

## Scope and validity

The method is described as suited to clocks designed for stability rather than accuracy: a cancellation tuned at one operating point removes the shift's effect without measuring its size or functional form. The demonstrated stability figures, the residual shift after compensation, and the operating conditions (cell temperature, power) are not transcribed here.

## Use in this record

This is the passive member of a family that also includes `andeweg2026` and `yudin2020`, which suppress the light shift by active power modulation rather than by canceling it against a second shift. A measurement of the AC-Stark ramp and its asymmetry, the quantity this analysis targets, is upstream of any scheme in this family: nulling a shift requires knowing its size and functional form first.
