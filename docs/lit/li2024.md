# li2024

**Held; abstract and introduction read 2026-07-31.** It had been sitting in
`PDF_papers/` without a lit file — surfaced by a scan for held PDFs with no
citekey, and it is the only such orphan sitting on this programme's core
subject.

## Why it matters to this analysis

This analysis *measures* an AC-Stark ramp and uses its asymmetry as a channel. This
paper *cancels* the light shift, in the same transition family, and bundles the
cell-temperature shift into the same trick. Verbatim from the abstract:

> "Light shift and vapor-cell temperature shift are the two most significant
> factors dominating the long-term instability of compact atomic clocks."

and their approach:

> "we propose a 'resonance-offset' locking approach that compensates for the two
> physical frequency shifts. In this approach, the additional offset locking
> shift can effectively counteract the atomic resonance shifts arising from
> changes in vapor-cell temperature and light power, reducing the net impact on
> the clock's frequency to nearly zero."

demonstrated "on the 778 nm Rubidium two-photon optical frequency standard".

**Two reasons to cite rather than ignore.** First, it is the *passive* member of
a family this repository already tracks — `andeweg2026` and `yudin2020` suppress
the light shift by active power modulation, this one by choosing the lock point
so the two shifts cancel. Second, it makes the metrological-practice case for
This analysis directly: the community's response to a light shift is to null it, which
is only possible once its size and functional form are known. A measurement of
the ramp is upstream of every scheme in this family.

**And it sharpens the limitation to state honestly.** Their own framing is that
the method suits clocks "designed for the excellent stability rather than
accuracy" — a cancellation tuned at one operating point does not tell you the
underlying shift, it hides it. That is the distinction this analysis should draw
explicitly.

## What has not been done

The demonstrated stability numbers, the residual shift after compensation, and
the operating conditions (cell temperature, power) are **not transcribed**, and
the bibliographic record is incomplete (see the verify flags). Both should be
settled before this is cited in a submitted draft.
