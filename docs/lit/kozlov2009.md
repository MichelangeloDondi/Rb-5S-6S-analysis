cited it for the co- against counter-propagating two-photon rate, it is
not that. It is the permutation-symmetry argument for which two-photon
components exist with identical photons, and the record's four F to F lines
(`constants.PEAKS`) are the J = 1/2 to J = 1/2 case of the scalar rule.
