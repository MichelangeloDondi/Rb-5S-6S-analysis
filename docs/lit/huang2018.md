# huang2018

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## The result

Two-step degenerate Raman sideband cooling in a two-dimensional optical
lattice, following Kerman and co-workers, with a fast stage into an auxiliary
state and a slow stage into a dark state, so the heating and cooling rates from
the optical pumping beam are controlled independently. The lattice runs at a
Lamb-Dicke parameter of 0.45, and the paper reports, verbatim, the "cooling of
spin-polarized" 85Rb "to the recoil temperature in both dimension within 2.4
ms" with the aid of adiabatic cooling.

## Why the isotope matters here

85Rb is one of the two isotopes this record's four lines come from, and the
isotope ratio is what several of its self-calibrations lean on. A cooling
scheme demonstrated on 85Rb rather than on 87 is directly usable.

## What it contributes to the platform question

Speed. A cold platform's cost against a warm cell is atom number per shot, and
that cost is paid back by repetition only if the preparation is short. Two and
a half milliseconds of cooling sits inside any cycle this record's forecast
models, so the limiting time is the trap loading and not the cooling.
