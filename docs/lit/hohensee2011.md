# hohensee2011

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## What it is

The abstract opens, verbatim, "We study the use of atom interferometers as detectors" of gravitational waves, and the paper
studies the sources and the technology such an instrument needs.

## Why it is held

Lineage, and calibration of the audience. A group whose earlier work sized
kilometre-baseline detectors and whose current work is centimetres of fibre
reasons fluently at both scales. A proposal to them may state a scale
argument without belabouring it.
