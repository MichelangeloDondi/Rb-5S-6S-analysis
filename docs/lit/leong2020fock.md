# leong2020fock

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## The result

The abstract states, verbatim, that "we use the waveguide mode of a hollow-core photonic crystal fibre to manipulate the mechanical Fock states of single atoms in a harmonic potential inside the fibre".

## Why it bears on the guided arm

`xin2019` makes the residual differential light shift of the guiding trap
the limit on a guided coherence, and `wang2022` answers it with a shallower
trap. Both of those require the trap to be known. This paper is the
demonstration that it is: addressing motional Fock states in the guided
potential means its frequency and anharmonicity are measured, not assumed.
So a guided lineshape budget for this record's transition could carry the
trap's own shift as a known term rather than as an envelope.
