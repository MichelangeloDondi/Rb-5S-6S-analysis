# xin2025

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## What it is

An analysis of atom interferometers driven inside optical harmonic
potentials, published in 2025. Its abstract states, verbatim, "Efficient
methods are introduced to minimize the inhomogeneous broadening of oscillation
frequencies in atoms confined within a three-dimensional optical lattice
trap".

## Why it is held

Currency, and the affiliation. The author list carries the Department of
Physics, National Taiwan University, Taipei, which is where this record's
application is directed. The physics is the group's standing programme and
adds no number to this record's platform table.
