# chen2020

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## The measurement

The abstract states, verbatim, that the authors "use a dispersive measurement
of light passing through a moving room temperature atomic vapor cell to
determine the velocity of the cell in a single shot with a short-term
sensitivity of 5.5" micrometres per second per root hertz, with the dispersion
enhanced by quantum interference.

## Why it belongs in this record's platform question

The owner's question was what the host group's work says about extending this
experiment into fibres, traps and molasses. This paper is the reminder that
their programme also runs warm cells, and that a warm Doppler-broadened vapour
read in transmission is a platform they already operate.

The contrast with this record runs the other way. Their
sensitivity is obtained by making the medium's *dispersion* steep, which needs
the ensemble only to be numerous. This record's bound is limited by the
*widths*, and its campaign proposes to reduce them. A warm cell is the right
platform for the first and the wrong one for the second, and the two
programmes want opposite things from the same apparatus.

## The cold sibling

`kuan2016` is the same effect in a cold ensemble, where the abstract reports
an enhancement of the dragging "by at least three orders of magnitude" over
previous experiments and a velocimeter with "sensitivity two orders of
magnitude higher than the velocity width" of the medium.
