Held. Verified against the PDF.

## The system

Spectroscopic measurement of the Casimir-Polder force between ultracold Sr atoms and a dielectric surface, in the intermediate regime between the near-field and retarded limits. The shift is read from the atomic lineshape at the 189 nm Sr transition and is of order kHz.

## Use in this record

Serves as the template for extracting a near-surface shift from a spectroscopic lineshape, applied in the nanofiber extension, Section IV. The Rb D-line reference data used elsewhere in this record is by the same author, D. A. Steck.
