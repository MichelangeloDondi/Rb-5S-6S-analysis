## Use in this record

This is a provenance marker, not a technical source: there is nothing in a 60-word conference
abstract to derive a lineshape term or a number from. Its use is to date, in Hänsch's own words,
the framing of hydrogen two-photon precision spectroscopy as a programme aimed at fundamental
constants (the Rydberg constant, the proton/electron mass ratio, the proton and deuteron charge
radii) as of October 1986, contemporaneous with the Bordé (`borde1976`) transit-time treatments
and roughly a decade before the trajectory-resolved AC-Stark model (`haas2006`) and the later
hydrogen 1S-2S measurements (`parthey2011`, `matveev2013`) already on this shelf, all from
descendants of the same Garching programme. It fills the gap the owner's plan flagged (this
programme's hydrogen-1S-2S lineage should not visibly skip from Bordé 1976 to Fischer 2004 with no
marker in between) without overclaiming a technical link this single page cannot support. The
still-owed Beausoleil and Hänsch PRA 33, 1661 (1986) is the item that would actually carry
technical content from this era, and nothing here substitutes for it.
