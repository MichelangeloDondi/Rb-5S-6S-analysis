## What it says, in its own terms

This is a survey and outlook piece, not a new measurement: it reviews the optical-nanofibre (ONF) platform for interfacing single atoms or atomic ensembles with guided light, and states its own purpose as highlighting "recent advances, experimental techniques, and future perspectives of the optical nanofibre-atom hybrid quantum platform." It frames the ONF alongside other nanophotonic platforms for atom-photon coupling -- photonic-crystal waveguides, hollow-core photonic-crystal fibres, superconducting coplanar-waveguide resonators -- and argues the ONF's advantages are fibre-diameter homogeneity over several millimetres, near-lossless adiabatic coupling to standard fibre, and an evanescent field strong enough for near-field atom manipulation and probing, all without the complex nanofabrication the chip-based alternatives require.

The survey groups current results by which guided mode carries the interaction. Most work uses the fundamental HE11 mode: fluorescence-based atom probing and thermometry, electromagnetically-induced transparency in both room-temperature vapour and laser-cooled atoms at very low guided power, and ladder-type multilevel excitation schemes. Among the specific results it names in this category are guided-mode two-photon excitation of the Rb 87 5S1/2-6S1/2 transition and observation of the Rb 87 5S1/2-4D3/2 electric-quadrupole transition using 516.5 nm guided light, the latter flagged as having potential application to parity-nonconservation measurements. Beyond the fundamental mode, the review covers three alternative guided-mode strategies proposed or demonstrated for stronger atom-photon interaction: composite modes from two coupled parallel nanofibres, higher-order guided modes with longer evanescent penetration depth and larger fractional power outside the fibre, and modes engineered through nanostructuring (such as a slotted nanofibre).

Throughout, the review connects these results to waveguide quantum electrodynamics (wQED) as an organizing theme -- the regime where confined photonic modes give strong coupling between a quantum emitter and a propagating photonic state, in contrast to a closed cavity -- and to chiral atom-light interactions as a distinguishing feature of the ONF geometry.

## What it is worth here

This is background and orientation for the optical-nanofibre addendum, not a numerical source: it is a perspective piece surveying the field the group's own apparatus sits in, not a report of new data. Its concrete value to this record is pointing at the specific prior ONF results closest to this programme's own transition -- the guided-mode two-photon excitation of Rb 5S1/2-6S1/2 and the guided electric-quadrupole 5S1/2-4D3/2 line -- as citable precedents for what the fibre platform has already demonstrated on this exact atom and a neighbouring transition. Beyond that pointer, its relevance is mainly to the application-facing framing of the ONF addendum (what the platform offers, what it still lacks), not to any measurement this record performs.
