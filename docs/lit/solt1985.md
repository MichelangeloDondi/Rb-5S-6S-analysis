# rabinovich1962 (the file), solt1985 (the content)

Held under the wrong name, twenty-four pages, read in full against the

## What the file contains

G. Solt and H. Beck, "Linear electronic response of a dissolved impurity
and the interatomic forces in dilute metal hydrides", J. Phys. F: Met.
Phys. 15 (1985) 2085 to 2107, received 8 February 1985 (p. 2085). A
density-functional linear-response treatment of a hydrogen impurity in a
free-electron metal: the impurity susceptibility chi_l(r, r') by angular
momentum channel, the Friedel sum rule fixing a two-parameter square-well
impurity potential, the induced electron density around the proton, and
the impurity-induced host-host interaction and force constants, worked
for Al-H (Sections 2 to 5, pp. 2086 to 2104. Appendices pp. 2105 to
2107). Nothing in it concerns atomic vapours, spectroscopy, collisions or
vapour pressure.
