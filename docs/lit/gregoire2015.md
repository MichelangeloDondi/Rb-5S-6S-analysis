## What it says, in its own terms

The group measures the static electric-dipole ground-state polarizabilities
of Cs, Rb and K with a three-grating Mach-Zehnder atom-beam interferometer,
using phase choppers to measure the atomic beam's velocity distribution and
an electric-field-gradient interaction region (a pair of oppositely charged
cylindrical electrodes) in place of the septum electrode used in earlier
sodium work, so that a heavy, hard-to-resolve species such as Cs can be
measured without resolving individual diffraction orders. The field gradient
shifts the interference phase by an amount proportional to alpha/v0^2, and
the same apparatus is used for all three atoms, so most systematic effects
are common between them and largely cancel in the ratios, which come out
with smaller fractional uncertainty (0.08%) than the individual
polarizabilities (0.16%).

Beyond the headline polarizabilities, the same measurements are used to
extract the Cs 6p1/2 and 6p3/2, the Rb 5p1/2 and 5p3/2, and the K 4p1/2 and
4p3/2 state lifetimes and their associated electric-dipole matrix elements,
oscillator strengths and line strengths, plus van der Waals C6 coefficients.
Combined with published transition Stark shifts, the paper also reports
several excited-state polarizabilities to better than 0.09%. A stated
motivation is to benchmark the atomic-structure calculations that enter the
standard-model comparison in caesium atomic parity-nonconservation
experiments, through the 6s1/2 to 6p1/2 matrix element.

## What it is worth here

A direct, high-precision, independent measurement of the Rb ground-state
static polarizability (320.1(6) a.u.) and of the Rb 5p1/2 and 5p3/2
lifetimes, both of which are exactly the kind of number a sum-over-states
calculation of the Rb 5S and 6S dynamic polarizabilities would want as a
cross-check, even though the ground state alone is only one ingredient of
the 5S-6S differential polarizability. The paper says nothing about the
5S-6S transition, the ac-Stark ramp, or any two-photon lineshape physics, so
its relevance here is narrow: one candidate anchor constant, not prior art
on method.
