## What the abstract states

Quantum expressions for the cumulants of a continuous measurement exhibit the same simple
structure as those for the moments, and the application named is spin-noise spectroscopy.
That structural correspondence is the property a cumulant channel of a noisy signal rests on.

## What the abstract does not state

Three questions returned nothing from the page that was read: whether higher cumulants are
preferred to higher moments and why, whether estimating them from finite noisy data carries a
bias or a variance that grows with order, and any expression for how an estimator's variance
grows with cumulant order.
