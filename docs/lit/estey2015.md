## What it is

The abstract states, verbatim, "We experimentally and theoretically study the diffraction phase of large-momentum transfer beam splitters in atom interferometers based on Bragg diffraction", and the authors null that
phase and raise the interferometer's sensitivity by combining Bragg
diffraction with Bloch oscillations.
