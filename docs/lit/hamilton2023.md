## The system and method

A retro-reflected two-photon transition in Rb-87 vapor, 5S₁/₂→5D₅/₂, driven by two colors (780 and 776 nm) through the near-resonant 5P intermediate state, distinct from the degenerate single-color I² transition at 993 nm (5S→6S). The signal is the two-color product I(r)_780 × I(r)_776, integrated over the transverse beam profile as F(Δ) = ∫ F(Δ,r) r dr with the cylindrical Jacobian. The paper reduces this integral to a single spatially averaged shift, related to the peak differential polarizability Δα by a factor of about 3.6, and does not retain the shift distribution. Axial standing-wave fringes are not treated: the average is over the transverse profile only.

## The numbers

A magic wavelength of 776.179 nm is reported for the 5D₅/₂ clock transition.

## Use in this record

The closed-form wedge distribution, f(s) ∝ |s|, and its third moment are absent from this reduction. The reported magic wavelength is the target of a proposed Ti:Sapphire asymmetry scan (`FUTURE_TRANSITIONS_titsapph.md`).
