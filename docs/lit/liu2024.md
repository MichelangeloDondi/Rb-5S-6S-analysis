# liu2024

**Read 2026-07-30** from the held arXiv v1 (2412.20734), fetched at the
experimenter's request. Fudan University (Liu, Wu, Wu), Shanxi University
(Jiang, Zhao). See [patterson2018](patterson2018.md) for the full analysis
against the mechanism proposed there — this note carries the bibliographic
record and the passages it depends on.

## The system

Cold ⁸⁷Rb atoms near an optical nanofibre, held with **ferromagnetic traps**
enabling quasi-continuous operation (the paper's main subject is the trapping
scheme; the linewidth measurement is one application). A **nanosecond** probe,
so "the atomic motion is effectively frozen" during the measurement and the
density is treated as largely uniform in the near field.

## The numbers, verbatim

> we arrive at a fitted linewidth of $\tilde\Gamma/2\pi \approx 9.5$–$10.5$
> MHz. This value is substantially larger than one might expect from the
> $\Gamma = 2\pi \times 6.1$ MHz natural linewidth of the D2 transition, even
> after accounting for the ONF-enhanced emission and the surface interactions

and, on the lineshape itself:

> that we see a Lorenzian absorption profile ... broadened but without
> substantial van-der-Waals-shift induced asymmetry ... is quite puzzling.

## Their own explanation, which is a rival, not a confirmation

> we ... provide strong evidence that the anomalously large linewidth ... is
> affected by the surface quality of ONF ... and is likely of magnetic origin.

That is not the position-dependent-decay-rate mechanism proposed in
[patterson2018](patterson2018.md); it is a distinct candidate (surface quality,
magnetic) that happens to share the same qualitative signature — symmetric
broadening surviving the known corrections. **The two have not been
distinguished.** Reading their Appendix C (referenced for the supporting
evidence) is the next step.
