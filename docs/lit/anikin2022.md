# anikin2022

Held, the arXiv version, REPORTED because only its contents page and title
were read here. The record's use of it is as the reference for the general
statement that a symmetric profile fitted to an asymmetric line returns a
centre displaced by an amount set by the asymmetry's origin, which the
review develops for quantum-interference and nonresonant asymmetries in
hydrogen and helium.

## What it is not

The asymmetry of this record's line is the inhomogeneous light shift over
the beam's intensity distribution (the ramp), not the quantum interference
between resonances the review treats, the review's shifts are of order the
natural width times a ratio of matrix elements, and this record's is of
order the light shift itself. The review bears on the method (the Fano-Voigt
and related asymmetric forms, and the statement that the asymmetry
parameter correlates with the fitted centre) and not on the number.
