A review summarising the second workshop on terrestrial very-long-baseline
atom interferometry, with several hundred authors. It says of itself, verbatim,
"It documents our concerted efforts to evaluate progress, address emerging
challenges, and refine strategic directions for future large-scale atom
interferometry projects".

measurement, and no number in it bears on a two-photon linewidth.
