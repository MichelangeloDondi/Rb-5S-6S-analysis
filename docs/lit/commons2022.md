# commons2022

Held, REPORTED. The paper's own framing, verbatim from its introduction:
"Frequency sweeps slower than internal timescales are used in measurements of
line centers, widths, and lineshape asymmetries", while faster sweeps, in
its words, "excite other co-operative processes that modulate the observed
optical response". The archive's 40-second sweeps over under 100 MHz are the slow
regime, the chirp asymmetry the paper measures belongs to the fast one, and
the record's asymmetry has a different origin (the light-shift ramp).
