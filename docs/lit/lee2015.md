VERIFIED. Held (arXiv v2). Read in full on 2026-09-22.

## What it does

The nanofibre has a 530 nm diameter waist over 7 mm. A 750 nm blue-detuned travelling wave (7.4 mW) and a 1064 nm red-detuned standing wave (3.35 mW per beam) make a trap 370 uK deep about 215 nm from the surface, with calculated frequencies (253, 371, 104) kHz (Section II. The two beam powers are from Fig. 2's caption). Atoms loaded from a 15 uK molasses live about 23 ms. Absorption of a sub-pW 780 nm probe sent through the fibre is photon-counted, with volume Bragg gratings filtering the blue beam's background (Sections II and III).

The measured profile (Fig. 2) is strongly asymmetric. Section IV traces the asymmetry to the trap's light shifts. Lower temperature moves the profile blue, because the atoms sit deeper. The red wing is set by the Boltzmann truncation. The fundamental mode's longitudinal field makes the polarization elliptical, and the resulting vector light shift widens the blue side, more in Rb than in Cs because of Rb's larger excited-state vector shifts (Fig. 3). The model, Eqs. (2) to (5), weights the light-shifted transition frequency by a truncated Boltzmann factor over the trap volume. Each position's Lorentzian is widened by the spread of the state-dependent shifts there, and the fraction of circular polarization is a free mixing ratio (Fig. 4).

## The numbers

- Atom number from the tails of the profile: N = 302 at T = 55 uK, which is an optical depth of 8.4 with a single-atom optical depth of 0.0278 (Section IV).
- Lower bound from the deepest absorption, 96.8 per cent at 10 MHz detuning: N = 123.
- The model misses a dip in absorption near 15 MHz, which the authors suggest may come from probe heating or probe-induced optical pumping (Section IV).

## Use in this record

- The nanofibre-arm precedent: an asymmetric profile produced by the distribution of light shifts that a trapped ensemble samples, modelled and used to extract atom number and, qualitatively, temperature. The treatment is a full-profile model with a free polarization ratio, with no moments and no stated uncertainty on the extracted numbers.
- A term for the nanofibre model to answer before it prices anything: the longitudinal field component makes the evanescent polarization elliptical, and the vector part of the light shift is what distorts the line here. Whether the 5S1/2 and 6S1/2 vector polarizabilities give a differential vector shift that matters at the nanofibre intensities is a question for the record's polarizability code. It is not settled here.
- The two-colour trap geometry and the trap's light shifts are the object Síle's programme works with. The model is written per state pair and summed over Zeeman sublevels, which is the structure a nanofibre version of this record's volume model would need.

## Limits

- Qualitative agreement only, by the authors' own description. The polarization at the trap site and the m_F populations are uncertain, and a truncated Boltzmann density is assumed, not tested.
- The quoted atom numbers carry no uncertainty.
