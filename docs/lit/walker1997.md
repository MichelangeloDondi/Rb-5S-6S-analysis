# walker1997

Held, fourteen pages, read in full against the PDF on 2026-09-14. Table II
read twice.

## What it covers

The physics of transferring electron-spin polarisation from optically
pumped alkali atoms to noble-gas nuclei: optical pumping (Sec. III),
spin-exchange rate equations and photon efficiency (Sec. IV), the
spin-dependent interactions that set the cross sections (Sec. V), and the
competing relaxation channels, alkali-alkali collisions among them
(Sec. VI).

## The claims, with pages

1. Alkali-alkali spin-exchange cross sections are large, typically
   2e-14 cm^2 (p. 632, Sec. III.B), and under pumping conditions the
   spin-exchange rate is 1e4 to 1e6 s^-1 (p. 632).
2. Spin exchange conserves the total spin of the colliding pair and
   redistributes population among the ground sublevels toward the
   spin-temperature distribution, eqs. (4) to (7) and Fig. 5 (p. 632). The hyperfine interaction acts between collisions, not during them.
3. Table II (p. 640): measured spin-destruction cross sections, Cs-Cs
   2.03e-16, Rb-Rb 1.6e-17, K-K 2.4e-18 cm^2 (Bhaskar et al. 1980. Knize
   1989), attributed to a tensor S.(3RR-1).S interaction whose origin is
   not understood (p. 640).
4. Rubidium cells are run at 80 to 130 C, giving 1e11 to 1e14 cm^-3
   (p. 630), the same window as this record's archive.
5. Buffer-gas absorption linewidths are typically 30 GHz or more above
   one amagat, 2.69e19 cm^-3 (p. 631 and footnote 2), against a largest
   ground hyperfine interval of 9 GHz in Cs.
6. The Fermi pseudopotential, eq. (23), and the noble-gas scattering
   lengths of Table I (p. 636): He 0.63, Ne 0.13, Ar -0.90, Kr -1.96,
   Xe -3.4 A, the mechanism behind the pressure shift of alkali Rydberg
   states.
7. Rb-N2 spin-relaxation cross section 1.2e-22 cm^2 (p. 639).
8. Summary (p. 641): at very high temperature an electron-spin
   destruction rate proportional to the alkali density is observed that
   disagrees seriously with theory.

## Validity

A review. The numbers above are quoted from the primary sources it names
and are not re-measured. Claim 1 is a typical value, and the measured
Rb85-Rb87 value is [jarrett1964](jarrett1964.md)'s 1.70e-14.

## What this record takes from it

1. Confirmation of the scale of the spin-exchange coefficient
   [jarrett1964](jarrett1964.md) supplies for the four-peak F contrasts,
   and the statement of what the collisions conserve, which fixes the sign
   of any repopulation term: they do not restore thermal populations
   across F but conserve total spin, so the level the two-photon drive
   empties is refilled only in proportion to what the other level can
   give while keeping the pair's spin.
2. The electron-spin randomising channel is negligible here: with the
   Rb-Rb value of Table II and the record's density at 130 C the rate is
   2.9e13 times 1.6e-17 times 4.4e4 cm/s = 21 s^-1 (calculated here), five
   orders below the transit rate.
3. Nothing bears on beta_self, the density law or the waist. The
   buffer-gas material is out of scope for a sealed cell and is held only
   so that a reader who asks about foreign-gas broadening finds the
   regime stated: 30 GHz per amagat is the scale, and the record's cell
   has no buffer gas.
