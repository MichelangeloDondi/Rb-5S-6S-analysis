# xin2019

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## The result

Coherent guiding of ground-state superpositions of 85Rb over a centimetre
range and hundreds of milliseconds inside a four-centimetre hollow-core
photonic crystal fibre. The abstract states, verbatim, "The decoherence is mainly
due to dephasing from the residual differential light shift from the optical
trap and the inhomogeneity of an ambient magnetic field", and the body says
the coherence time was extended "by mitigating decoherence from the
inhomogeneous broadening of the differential light shift (DLS)".

## Why it matters to this record

This repository's standing condition is that a convolution holds only where
the homogeneous kernel is the same at every collected volume element, and that
the vapour cell fails it twice at a tight waist because the transit width and
the saturation companion both key on the local intensity.

**This paper's inhomogeneity does not by itself break that condition.** A
spread of *centres* across the mode is a mixture of identically shaped kernels at
different positions, which is exactly what a convolution is. The condition is
about the kernel's *width* depending on the mixture variable, and not about its
centre. So the guided trap's
varying light shift is an inhomogeneous broadening the convolution form already
handles. What would break the condition in a guided arm is the same thing that
breaks it in the cell: the saturation companion, whose width keys on the local
probe intensity, and which varies across the mode for the same geometric
reason. This paper's contribution here is the trap depth as a line-shape
parameter and the measured size of the dephasing, not the convolution
condition.

The consequence for the guided arm of the campaign is that its trap depth is a
line-shape parameter and not only a loading parameter. A deeper trap holds
more atoms and broadens more, and the two pull against each other in the same
direction the cell's waist does.

## The mitigation the group itself uses

<!-- not-from-pdf: the quotation below is wang2022's, verified verbatim in its
     own note against its own PDF. It is repeated here for the reader and must

Cooling inside the fibre, so a shallower trap suffices. That is `wang2022`,
which reports in-fibre gray molasses and delta-kick cooling and says in as
many words that the cooling "allows a shallow guiding optical potential to
minimize decoherence". So the host group's own answer to the differential
light shift is colder atoms rather than a cleverer kernel, and this record's
guided forecast should price the trap depth rather than assume it.
