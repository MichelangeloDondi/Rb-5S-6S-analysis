# feng2026

**Read here 2026-08-03.** Peking University (Institute of Quantum Electronics
+ State Key Laboratory for Artificial Microstructure and Mesoscopic Physics).

## Verbatim abstract

> "We report a rubidium optical clock based on a new architecture in which a
> fiber laser is stabilized to the two-photon 5S1/2(F = 1) → 5D5/2(F′ = 1)
> transition of 87Rb, a choice that reduces the external field sensitivity
> relative to commonly used transitions. Long-term frequency fluctuation is
> minimized by equilibrating the helium concentration in the vapor cell,
> thereby suppressing collisional shifts from helium permeation. The error
> signal is generated through third-harmonic demodulation for the suppression
> of the residual amplitude modulation. With these measures, the system
> reaches a fractional frequency instability of 3.5 × 10−13 at 1 s and
> 6.6 × 10−15 at 10,000 s. Its long-term stability matches that of a VCH-1008
> passive hydrogen maser over the same averaging time, demonstrating the
> potential of this compact rubidium optical clock for precision timekeeping
> and navigation."

## What it does

Drives the two-photon transition 5S1/2(F=1) → 5D5/2(F'=1) of isotopically
enriched 87Rb at 778.1 nm (SHG, in periodically poled lithium niobate, of a
1556.2 nm fibre-DFB laser), 30 mW, 1 mm waist radius. Reads out on the 420 nm
fluorescence of the 5D→6P→5S decay cascade (PMT, Hamamatsu H10722-210), the
same cascade-detection family as [beard2024](beard2024.md)'s 776 nm channel.
A liquid-crystal variable retarder stabilizes the PMT voltage for laser-power
control in place of a dedicated photodiode.

**The transition choice is the first passive lever.** Most prior 5S-5D clocks
use F=2 → F'=4, which has many Zeeman sublevels and is broadened by residual
milligauss fields and distorted by tensor ac-Stark shifts. F=1 → F'=1 has
smaller angular momentum and simpler structure: the tensor ac-Stark shift of
5D5/2(F'=1) is stated as **nearly 3.6 times smaller** than that of
5D5/2(F'=4). This is a structural/hyperfine-selection suppression of the
*mean* shift, not an active servo and not a magic wavelength.

**The second lever is 3f-demodulation**, demodulating the AOM-modulated
fluorescence at the third harmonic of the 30 kHz modulation tone (90 kHz)
rather than the modulation frequency itself, as a passive way to suppress the
baseline shift of the error signal from residual amplitude modulation (RAM).
An AOM is used specifically because it has much less RAM than an EOM (no
birefringence contribution); the cell is additionally tilted 11° to suppress
the parasitic-etalon RAM channel. The modulation depth (2β) has no value that
cancels RAM completely (Fig. 4) and is instead tuned empirically to 9.76 to
trade off error-signal slope against residual frequency pulling.

**The third lever is helium-permeation equilibration**, the paper's headline
mechanism. Ambient helium diffuses through the borosilicate glass cell over
time (Eq. 1, a cylindrical diffusion model with inner radius a=11 mm, outer
radius b=12 mm, length L=50 mm, permeability K=6×10⁻⁷ mm²/s), giving a
theoretical time constant T ≈ 8 days. The cell was baked at 358 K (85 °C) for
**over 300 days — roughly 37 time constants** — before data were taken, so
the internal He partial pressure has equilibrated with the ambient
concentration rather than still climbing toward it. Result: "no resolvable
linear frequency drift observed in this work compared to previous
demonstrations," where earlier work on this line did see such drift. Future
work proposes aluminosilicate-glass cells (lower He permeability) to further
damp the residual daily fluctuation of ambient He concentration, and either a
dual-interrogation method or a portion of the high-power 1556.2 nm seed for
further ac-Stark suppression.

## Headline numbers

- **Fractional frequency instability:** 3.5×10⁻¹³ at 1 s (short-term);
  **6.6×10⁻¹⁵ at 10,000 s** (long-term) — the paper's headline result, and the
  number in its title.
- **Matches a VCH-1008 passive hydrogen maser** over the same averaging range
  (Fig. 5b), referenced against an active hydrogen maser VCH-1003M
  (5×10⁻¹⁴ @ 1 s, 5×10⁻¹⁶ @ 1 day) via a commercial optical frequency comb.
- **Linewidth Γ = 670 kHz FWHM** (natural linewidth 330 kHz + collisional
  broadening), a factor-~2 broadening above the bare natural width.
- **Helium equilibration:** cell at 358 K for >300 days, ≈8-day theoretical
  permeation time constant (cylindrical borosilicate cell, Eq. 1) — ~37×
  overbake to reach equilibrium.
- **Tensor ac-Stark shift of 5D5/2(F'=1) ≈ 1/3.6** that of the commonly used
  5D5/2(F'=4).
- **Cell/optics details:** 778.1 nm drive (30 mW, 1 mm waist), 420 nm cascade
  fluorescence detection, 11° cell tilt, AOM modulation at 30 kHz / 3f-demod
  at 90 kHz, modulation depth 2β = 9.76 (empirical optimum).

## Bridges to this repo

**778 nm competitive-landscape positioning (`docs/LITERATURE.md`'s "778 nm
5S→5D clock frontier" / `BIG_PICTURE.md` §1).** `feng2026` is a third,
independent entry in the compact 778 nm 5S-5D clock ecosystem our 993 nm
5S-6S programme is explicitly delineated against — alongside
[andeweg2026](andeweg2026.md) (NIST, active power-modulation ac-Stark
suppression ×1000) and [ahern2025](ahern2025.md) (Adelaide, two-colour
5S-5D, 6×10⁻¹⁴/√τ, light-shift-limited). `BIG_PICTURE.md` already states
that on natural linewidth 993 nm "starts *behind*" this line (330 kHz vs.
6S's 3.49 MHz natural width) and that the field's suppression effort —
shift cancellation, ×1000 power modulation, magic wavelengths — "suppresses
the **mean** shift", in contrast to this repo's reference-free shape/asymmetry
method. `feng2026` adds a genuinely new axis to that list rather than
duplicating it: it suppresses the mean *tensor* ac-Stark shift not by an
active servo or a magic wavelength but by **hyperfine sub-level selection**
(F=1→F'=1 over F=2→F'=4), and it separately attacks **long-term He-buffer
drift**, a systematic neither Andeweg nor Ahern's notes discuss. None of the
three groups works 993 nm; all three (now four with `feng2026`) reinforce
that 778 nm 5S-5D, not 993 nm 5S-6S, is the compact-clock community's
entrenched line, and that 6.6×10⁻¹⁵ @ 10⁴ s matching a passive maser is the
long-term bar this repo's own line is not attempting to clear.

**Self-broadening bound (`weller2011.md`, β_self).** Not the same physics,
and worth stating plainly so the two are never conflated: `feng2026`'s
controlled shift is a **foreign-gas (He) collisional shift** from a buffer
gas that leaks in from the ambient atmosphere through the glass wall, not
the **Rb-Rb resonant/van-der-Waals self-broadening** this repo's β_self
characterizes. `docs/APPARATUS.md` describes our own cell only as a "glass
vapour cell in a copper block" with no documented glass composition or
seal/fill date. That is an open question worth flagging rather than a
confirmed problem: He permeation through borosilicate happens whether or not
He is an intentional buffer gas, so an old, sealed, borosilicate cell could
in principle be accumulating an undocumented He collisional-broadening
component alongside the Rb-Rb term this programme fits — exactly the kind of
slow, unmodelled addition that would bias a β_self extraction or masquerade
as drift. Worth a line in `APPARATUS.md` confirming the cell's glass type
and fill date if not already on record.

**Drift-immune methods (M20 / third-cumulant lineage).** `feng2026` solves a
long-term-drift problem with a philosophy opposite to this repo's own: it
engineers the drift away *before* the data are taken (bake the cell for
~37 permeation time constants until the internal He pressure stops moving),
where this programme's response to an uncontrolled drift knob has been
analysis-level — extracting a statistic (the third cumulant) that stays
valid despite the drift rather than removing the drift's physical cause.
Both are legitimate answers to the same class of problem and worth citing
as a contrast pair: hardware pre-equilibration vs. drift-immune statistics,
for whichever a future systematics section needs.

**Secondary methods note.** The 3f-demodulation RAM-suppression scheme (Eqs.
2-4, and the "no magic modulation depth" numerical result in Fig. 4) is a
transferable lock-in technique, independent of the 5D line — worth a look if
RAM is ever a suspect systematic in the 993 nm error signal.
