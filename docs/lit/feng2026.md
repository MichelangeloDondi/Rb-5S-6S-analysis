# feng2026

Held. Feng, Luo, Lyu, Yu, Qi, Yang, and Wang, Peking University (Institute of Quantum Electronics and State Key Laboratory for Artificial Microstructure and Mesoscopic Physics).

## The system

Drives the two-photon transition 5S1/2(F=1) -> 5D5/2(F'=1) of isotopically enriched 87Rb at 778.1 nm, generated by second-harmonic generation in periodically poled lithium niobate from a 1556.2 nm fibre-DFB laser, at 30 mW with a 1 mm waist radius. Readout is on the 420 nm fluorescence of the 5D -> 6P -> 5S decay cascade (PMT, Hamamatsu H10722-210). A liquid-crystal variable retarder stabilizes the PMT voltage for laser-power control in place of a dedicated photodiode.

## Method

Most prior 5S-5D clocks use F=2 -> F'=4, which has many Zeeman sublevels, is broadened by residual milligauss fields, and is distorted by tensor AC-Stark shifts. F=1 -> F'=1 has smaller angular momentum and simpler structure: the tensor AC-Stark shift of 5D5/2(F'=1) is nearly 3.6 times smaller than that of 5D5/2(F'=4), a structural, hyperfine-selection suppression of the mean shift rather than an active servo or a magic wavelength.

A second suppression is 3f-demodulation: the AOM-modulated fluorescence is demodulated at the third harmonic of the 30 kHz modulation tone (90 kHz) rather than at the modulation frequency itself, passively suppressing the baseline shift of the error signal from residual amplitude modulation. An AOM is used because it produces much less residual amplitude modulation than an EOM, which has a birefringence contribution. The cell is additionally tilted 11 degrees to suppress the parasitic-etalon modulation channel. The modulation depth (2β) has no value that cancels the residual modulation completely and is instead tuned empirically to 9.76 to trade error-signal slope against residual frequency pulling.

The third suppression is helium-permeation equilibration. Ambient helium diffuses through the borosilicate glass cell over time. A cylindrical diffusion model (inner radius 11 mm, outer radius 12 mm, length 50 mm, permeability 6x10^-7 mm^2/s) gives a theoretical time constant of about 8 days. The cell was baked at 358 K (85 °C) for over 300 days, roughly 37 time constants, before data were taken, so the internal helium partial pressure had equilibrated with the ambient concentration rather than still climbing toward it. No resolvable linear frequency drift was observed, where earlier work on this line had shown such drift.

## The numbers

- Fractional frequency instability: 3.5x10^-13 at 1 s, 6.6x10^-15 at 10,000 s.
- Matches a VCH-1008 passive hydrogen maser over the same averaging range, referenced against an active hydrogen maser VCH-1003M (5x10^-14 at 1 s, 5x10^-16 at 1 day) via a commercial optical frequency comb.
- Linewidth Γ = 670 kHz FWHM (natural linewidth 330 kHz plus collisional broadening), about a factor of 2 above the bare natural width.
- Helium equilibration: cell at 358 K for over 300 days against an approximately 8-day theoretical permeation time constant, roughly 37 time constants of overbake.
- Tensor AC-Stark shift of 5D5/2(F'=1) is about 1/3.6 that of the commonly used 5D5/2(F'=4).
- Cell and optics: 778.1 nm drive (30 mW, 1 mm waist), 420 nm cascade fluorescence detection, 11° cell tilt, AOM modulation at 30 kHz with 3f-demodulation at 90 kHz, modulation depth 2β = 9.76 (empirical optimum).

## Use in this record

The helium-permeation collisional shift this paper controls is a foreign-gas buffer effect from ambient helium permeating through the cell wall, distinct from the Rb-Rb resonant self-broadening coefficient characterized elsewhere in this record. The two are not to be conflated.
