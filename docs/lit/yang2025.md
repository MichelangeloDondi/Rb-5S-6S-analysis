# yang2025

**VERIFIED, 2026-07-17**, arXiv preprint (not Nature Photonics).

Integrated LiNbO3 electro-optic frequency combs track a laser's
instantaneous optical frequency in real time, resolving chirp rates up to
2e18 Hz/s. The method is an internal reference that follows the drifting
laser frequency directly, rather than comparing it against a fixed external
standard.
