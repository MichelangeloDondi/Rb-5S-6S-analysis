# lan2013

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## What it is

An atom interferometer used to link a clock's tick directly to a particle's
mass through the Compton frequency. It opens, verbatim, "Historically, time
measurements have been based on oscillation frequencies in systems of
particles, from the motion of celestial bodies to atomic transitions".

## Why it is held

Lineage. Together with `lan2012` and `estey2015` it dates the group's Berkeley
years under Mueller, which is where the light-pulse interferometry in the
later fibre work comes from.
