# lan2007

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## What it is

The abstract states, verbatim, "We propose and demonstrate an atomic qubit based on a cold 85Rb-87Rb isotopic mixture", entangled with a frequency-encoded
optical qubit.

## Why this one is not only lineage

This record's frequency axis is ruled by pairs of lines drawn from both
isotopes, and the ratio of their Doppler widths, the square root of the mass
ratio, is a parameter-free check it already uses. Every cold platform in
`results/platform_twins.csv` carries one species implicitly. A cold
two-isotope sample would carry the ruler and the thermometer onto the cold
platform with it, which is the one thing the cold rows lose against the
warm cell besides atom number. That this group has prepared such a mixture
is worth knowing before the ONF and fibre arms are priced again.
