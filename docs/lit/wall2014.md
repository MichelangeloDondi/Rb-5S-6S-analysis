# wall2014

**REPORTED from the external literature audit (2026-07-26), not read here.**
The audit read the full text and reports the mechanism verbatim: atoms near the
focus experience a wide range of intensities while those farther out experience
a narrow range and therefore "bunch up close to the unperturbed transition
frequency", producing spectra with a maximum at the unperturbed position and a
long high-frequency tail.

**Why it matters: this is a partial scoop of the distribution idea.** That
bunching argument is the density-of-states reasoning that produces a
ramp-shaped weight — i.e. someone has already kept the light-shift
*distribution* and read an asymmetric line from it, in 2014. What it does not
contain, per the audit, is the closed-form triangular weight, the I^2
two-photon weighting, or the use of the asymmetry to recover a shift when the
frequency reference is unusable.
