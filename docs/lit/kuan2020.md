# kuan2020

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## The result

Slow light under electromagnetically induced transparency measures the
motion of cold atoms in an optical lattice undergoing Bloch oscillation.
The abstract states, verbatim, "The motion of atoms is mapped out through the phase shift of light without perturbing the external and internal state of the atoms".

## What this record does not model

`rb5s6s.platforms` branches on two detection modes, fluorescence and
absorption. A dispersive readout is neither: it takes the phase of the
transmitted beam and leaves the population alone. On a cold platform,
where atom number is the binding constraint, a measurement that does not
consume the sample changes the arithmetic of how many shots a bound needs.
That is not a claim this record can make yet, and it is why the branch is
named here rather than added.
