# kischkel1991

Held. Read and verified in full against the PDF (19 pages).

> "Using Doppler-free two-photon excitation from the ground state 4f¹⁴6s² ¹S₀ the even-parity J = 2 levels of the 4f¹⁴6s7d and 4f¹⁴6s8d configurations as well as the 4f¹⁴6p², 4f¹³6s²6p and 4f¹³5d6s6p configurations between 44760.4 and 47341.8 cm⁻¹ in neutral ytterbium have been investigated with regard to the hyperfine structure of the excited states and the isotope shift of these transitions. The experimental results for the hyperfine splitting constants have been analysed regarding possible singlet-triplet and configuration mixing. The experimental values have been compared with theoretical results obtained in an ab initio calculation. A strong influence for the second-order hyperfine interaction could be established in both the 4f¹⁴6s7d ³D₂ and the 4f¹⁴6s10s ¹S₀ states."

## The system

Kischkel, Baumann and Kümmel, at the Physikalisches Institut in Tübingen, study neutral ytterbium (Z = 70). Doppler-free two-photon absorption is driven by a retroreflected single-mode cw ring dye laser (Coherent CR 699-21, Stilbene 3) inside a heated thermionic diode (about 1000 K, a few grams of metallic Yb plus 8 hPa neon buffer gas), with a Faraday rotator blocking optical feedback and lock-in detection at 78.5 Hz. Two-photon excitation from the 4f¹⁴6s² ¹S₀ ground state reaches the J=2 members of the 4f¹⁴6s7d, 4f¹⁴6s8d, 4f¹⁴6p², 4f¹³6s²6p and 4f¹³5d6s6p configurations, between 44760 and 47342 cm⁻¹ (421 to 451 nm, their Table 1). The companion paper, [kischkel1992](kischkel1992.md), extends the same apparatus and method to the 6s9d and 6s10d states.

## The numbers

Peak positions, obtained by fitting Lorentzian curves of equal width to the spectra (a Doppler-broadened Gaussian background could be omitted without loss of accuracy), give the A and B hyperfine constants of ¹⁷¹Yb and ¹⁷³Yb for each transition (Table 2) and the isotope shifts among all five even isotopes plus the two odd ones (Table 3). For 4f¹⁴6s7d ³D₂: A(¹⁷¹Yb) = 2622(7) MHz, A(¹⁷³Yb) = -821(2) MHz. The isotope shift δν(172-176) for that state is -736(15) MHz.

Second-order hyperfine corrections, needed because several J=2 levels sit close in energy to other J states of the same configuration, are read from King-plot deviations of the odd isotopes and cross-checked against an ab initio effective-tensor-operator calculation (Sandars and Beck 1965) built on MCDF radial hyperfine integrals. Section 6 finds an approximately 19 percent admixture of 4f¹⁴6p² ¹D₂ in the 4f¹⁴6s7d ¹D₂ state, and the 4f¹³6s²6p (5/2,3/2)₂ level close to pure jj coupling.

The observed linewidth, about 50 MHz, exceeds the natural width. The authors attribute this in part to possible pressure broadening in the interaction region of the thermionic diode (citing Grynberg and Cagnac 1977), without reporting a broadening rate or density dependence. No pressure-broadening or pressure-shift coefficient appears anywhere else in the paper.
