# leong2023

Held. Verified against the PDF on 2026-09-11.

## What it is

Two-mode squeezed states in atomic mechanical oscillators. The opening line
reads, verbatim, "Two-mode squeezed states, which are entangled states with bipartite quantum correlations in continuous-variable systems".

## Why it is held

Lineage and direction. The author list carries the Nanyang and the National
Taiwan University affiliations together, which dates the group's move. And
the subject is the group's centre of mass: quantum control of motion in a
confined geometry. This record's contribution to such a programme is a
spectroscopic observable on the same hardware, which is not what the group
does today.
