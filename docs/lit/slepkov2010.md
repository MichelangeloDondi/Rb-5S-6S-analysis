# slepkov2010

**REPORTED from the external literature audit (2026-07-26), not read here**
(the audit itself read only abstract and figure captions).

Reported content: models the AC-Stark shift of a saturated-absorption line
produced by the non-uniform radial intensity distribution of a guided mode,
comparing flat-top against Gaussian-core profiles; the mode non-uniformity is
found to broaden and steepen the line toward higher frequencies, and measured
shifts larger than a flat-top model predicts are fit by the simulated
non-uniform-mode lineshape.

**Why it matters: a second partial scoop, and in the guided geometry this
programme's PD entry targets.** It is one-photon saturated absorption, so there
is no I^2 weighting and no closed form -- but "keep the distribution, fit the
asymmetric line in a structured field" is demonstrably not new.

**ACTION: obtain the PDF.** Doubly relevant, since it is Rb spectroscopy inside
a hollow-core fibre.
