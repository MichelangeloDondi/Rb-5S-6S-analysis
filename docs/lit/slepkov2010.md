# slepkov2010

Held. Verified against the PDF.

## The system

Rb atoms in a hollow-core photonic bandgap fiber, with a room-temperature vapor generated by light-induced atomic desorption. Large AC Stark shifts, power broadening, and transit-time broadening are present even at nanowatt powers. The fiber cross-sectional area is on the order of 10^-7 cm^2, so 18 mW of desorption light gives a light intensity of 10^8 mW/cm^2. That area corresponds to a mode radius of about 1.8 um (calculated as sqrt(A/pi)).

Two fiber core sizes appear in the paper's own lineage: 6 um for earlier Rb/acetylene EIT work, and 10 um for the acetylene saturation results it cites.

## The method

The signal is one-photon saturated absorption, so the weight is intensity I rather than the I^2 of a degenerate two-photon line. There is no triangular law and no closed form. The AC Stark shift is obtained from a numerical simulation of the guided-mode radial intensity profile rather than an analytic weight, and is used to correct a measured shift with the frequency axis intact.

## Use in this record

Establishes prior art for keeping a non-uniform intensity distribution and fitting the asymmetric line it produces, in preference to a uniform-intensity model. The method here corrects a measured shift with the frequency reference intact. It does not invert an asymmetric lineshape to recover a coefficient when no reference is available.
