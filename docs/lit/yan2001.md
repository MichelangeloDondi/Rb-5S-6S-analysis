## What it says, in its own terms

**The scheme.** A four-level atomic system, realised in cold 87Rb, is arranged so a coupling
laser (D1, F=2 -> F'=1) creates two dressed states from the excited manifold, a weak probe
(D1, F=1 -> F'=1) forms a standard Lambda-type EIT configuration with the coupling laser, and
an independent switching laser (D2, F=2 -> F'=3) addresses a fourth level. At line centre,
single-photon absorption of the probe interferes destructively and vanishes, while two-photon-
type absorption through the dressed states interferes constructively and is strongly
enhanced. A dressed-state argument (p. 3) shows the peak nonlinear absorption can approach
half the resonant linear-absorption amplitude of an isolated two-level system, a large
nonlinear response at low light levels compared with ordinary saturated linear absorption.

**The demonstration.** Because the two-photon-type absorption is proportional to the
switching laser's intensity while the single-photon channel stays suppressed, turning the
switching laser on and off toggles the probe's absorption: the paper reports this both for a
continuous-wave probe (switching-pulse duration varied from 1 to 200 us) and for a pulsed
probe, in a sequential measurement cycle (99.4 ms cooling and trapping, a 0.6 ms window for
the coupling/switching/probe interaction) run at a 10 Hz repetition rate in a vapour-cell MOT
holding about 1e8 87Rb atoms in a roughly 2 mm cloud at about 1e-9 Torr.

## What it is worth here

Little. This is a cold-atom, EIT-based nonlinear-optics demonstration, sharing a species
(87Rb) with this record but no transition, no observable and no experimental regime: it uses
a laser-cooled MOT sample and D-line (5S-5P) single-photon transitions to realise a four-level
interference scheme, where this record uses a warm vapour cell and a direct 5S-6S two-photon
transition to measure a self-broadened, AC-Stark-shifted lineshape. It is retained only as
background on cold-Rb EIT-based nonlinear optics, relevant at most to a separate EIT-focused
thread of this programme rather than to the vapour-cell 5S-6S analysis itself.
