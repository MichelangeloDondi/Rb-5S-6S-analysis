# kuhr2005

VERIFIED. Held (arXiv v2). Sections I to V A read in full on 2026-09-22.

## What it does

Cs atoms (1 to 50) sit in a 1064 nm standing-wave trap with a 40 um beam diameter (2 w0) and up to 2 W per beam, typically 1 mK deep, and are driven on the
|F=4, m_F=0> to |F=3, m_F=0> microwave clock transition (Section II). The
differential light shift is a fixed fraction of the trap depth, hbar delta0 = eta U0 with eta = omega_hfs / Delta_eff = 1.45e-4, so delta0 is -2pi x 3.0 kHz at 1 mK (Eqs. 11-12).

The inhomogeneous model (Section IV A) takes the three-dimensional Boltzmann energy distribution in a harmonic trap, p(E) proportional to E^2 exp(-E/kB T) (Eq. 13). It uses the virial theorem to set each atom's time-averaged potential energy at E/2 (Eq. 14), which maps the energy distribution onto a Gamma(3) distribution of differential shifts with scale 1/K, K = 2 hbar / (eta kB T) (Eqs. 15-16). The paper states the consequence in words: the Ramsey fringe is the Fourier (cosine) transform of the atomic energy distribution (after Eq. 23). The analytic result has an amplitude [1 + 0.95 (t/T2*)^2]^(-3/2) and a phase shift -3 arctan(0.97 t/T2*) (Eqs. 26-27), with T2* = 0.97 K, inversely proportional to the temperature (Eq. 28). The paper attributes the phase shift to the asymmetry of the shift distribution: hot atoms dephase faster, which weights the surviving mean toward larger negative shifts.

Sources: Table I (Ramsey) and Table II (echo). The fitted differential shifts, -268(13) Hz and -78(13) Hz, return trap depths of 0.090(4) mK and 0.026(4) mK. The estimates from power and waist are 0.1 and 0.04 mK, uncertain by up to 50 per cent. The paper attributes the low-depth gap to hot atoms lost when the trap is lowered, which truncates the distribution at E = U0 (Section IV A, after Eq. 31).

## Use in this record

- The conjugate-domain form of this record's programme, already standard for trapped atoms. With a shift distribution whose characteristic function is phi(t), the Ramsey contrast is |phi(t)| and the fringe phase is arg phi(t). For a Gamma(3) law these are exactly Eqs. (26)-(27): (1 + t^2/K^2)^(-3/2) and -3 arctan(t/K). The odd cumulants sit in the phase and the even ones in the contrast. The paper states the transform and the asymmetry-to-phase link, not the cumulant language. The rule file's statement that a frequency window is a sinc convolution of the characteristic function has here its time-domain counterpart.
- Self-calibration precedent: the atoms return the trap depth, 0.090(4) mK, against a power-and-waist estimate uncertain by 50 per cent. That is the same move this record makes with S0 and the waist, made in the time domain.
- For a guided-atom interferometer the canonical model is this one. The echo removes the static distribution, and what survives is fluctuation, which Eqs. (38)-(45) treat as a Gaussian detuning noise between the two echo halves. The part of the shift that changes along each atom's trajectory, which this record's transit and chirp machinery computes, is what such an echo does not refocus.

## Limits

- The virial-theorem mapping holds only for kB T much smaller than U0, in a harmonic potential, as the paper states after Eq. (16). The shift time-average is exact only for whole oscillation periods between pulses. The paper checks that the remainder is negligible.
- The inhomogeneous model is semiclassical. The paper reports a density-matrix check agreeing within one per cent, not presented.
