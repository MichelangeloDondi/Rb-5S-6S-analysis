# saha2010

Held. Verified against the arXiv v1 PDF. The published Phys. Rev. A version has not been checked against it.

## The system

Cornell, Gaeta group, in the same lineage as [Slepkov 2010](slepkov2010.md). Reports degenerate, Doppler-free two-photon absorption on the Rb 5S1/2 -> 5D5/2 transition at 778.1 nm, inside a hollow-core photonic band-gap fibre, with the 420 nm 6P3/2 cascade fluorescence as the monitor. The vapour is generated in-fibre by light-induced atomic desorption. The fibre is a 9 cm long, 6 µm diameter hollow-core photonic band-gap fibre (Crystal Fiber AIR-6-800), guiding 750-810 nm.

## The numbers

Pump absorption of 1% at a total power of 1 mW.

## Validity

The paper measures absorption depth. It gives no lineshape decomposition and no light-shift treatment. The guided-mode intensity distribution, shown in Slepkov 2010 to broaden and steepen the one-photon line, is present in this two-photon geometry as well and is not modelled here.

## Use in this record

This is the existence proof for two-photon absorption of an alkali line in a hollow-core fibre at milliwatt total power, the sister geometry and sister line to Slepkov 2010's one-photon saturated-absorption result.
