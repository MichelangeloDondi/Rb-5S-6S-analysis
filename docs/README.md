# The documentation, and where to start

Three doors, by what you came for. Before any of them, if the vocabulary is
new: [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md) explains the measurement in six sentences and
then defines every term and symbol used here, each with a pointer to where it
is derived.

**The physics.** [BIG_PICTURE.md](BIG_PICTURE.md) is the programme in one
document: what was measured, what is bounded, and why the bounds are the
result. [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md) is the generated ledger of record, every
headline number read from its producing CSV. [CLAIMS.md](CLAIMS.md) is
the claim register: each claim with its status, its evidence, and its
audience. [UNCERTAINTY.md](UNCERTAINTY.md) is how to read an error bar
here: what each provenance tag and status word licenses, which errors are
statistical and which are not, how the four bound constructions differ, and
where an uncertainty is deliberately absent.

**The method.** [methods.md](methods.md) is the umbrella over the eight
chapters in [methods/](methods/), from the measurement to the
assumptions. [THEORY_NOTE.md](THEORY_NOTE.md) carries the lineshape
theory. [DATA.md](DATA.md) is the data of record: campaign chronology,
exclusion register, the frequency ruler, and the bound history.
[APPARATUS.md](APPARATUS.md) is the bench, every fact tagged with its
provenance.

**The process.** [PREREGISTRATION_RESULTS.md](PREREGISTRATION_RESULTS.md)
holds the preregistrations and their thirty dated addenda, including
what failed. [RESEARCH_DECISIONS.md](RESEARCH_DECISIONS.md) records the
decisions and the mistakes they answer. [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) is the
forward plan. [STYLE.md](STYLE.md) is the writing contract this
documentation is held to, mechanically.

Reference layers: [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md) for every term and symbol,
[wiki/](wiki/README.md) for one page per concept, method, effect and
technique, with the theory, its use here and its failure modes,
[tests/README.md](../tests/README.md) for how every result is held to what
it claims, guard by guard, and
[REPRODUCING.md](REPRODUCING.md) for what runs from a clone and what needs
data that is not in it,
[LITERATURE.md](LITERATURE.md) with its
[index](LITERATURE_INDEX.md) and per-paper notes under [lit/](lit/),
working notes under [notes/](notes/),
[ADAPTING.md](ADAPTING.md) for reusing the pipeline on other data, and
[FUTURE_TRANSITIONS_titsapph.md](FUTURE_TRANSITIONS_titsapph.md), a
survey of physics options beyond this campaign.

**Conventions this record publishes so a reader can check them:**
[RELEASE_NOTE_STYLE.md](RELEASE_NOTE_STYLE.md) sets the form every release
note must satisfy, and the note checker refuses its mechanical half.
