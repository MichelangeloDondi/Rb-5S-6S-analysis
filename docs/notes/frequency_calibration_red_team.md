Verdict: CONFIRMED at investigation, **REFUTED** after adjudication. Severity: note.

**Adjudication.** The grid arithmetic is right (mirror of slot n lands at 2a-n, integer iff 2a is integer, so half-integer apexes are benign too), but no PUBLISHED text states the condition as an iff. **Action.** Not the factor of two. Write the corrected reading into docs/DATA.md section 7 itself: the structural argument is true of the ramp and false of a rigid-grid fit, whose window assignment is what a fold displaces, and an apex landing on a tooth (or, immaterially, on a half-tooth) is the benign case.
